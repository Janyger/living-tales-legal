Data Deletion

As of: October 2026

Living Tales is built to be data-minimal. Here is what data is stored where, and how you delete it.

1. Local data (on your device)

Your books live exclusively on your device: generated chapters, audio files, cover and portrait images, app settings, and library/progress data.

  • Delete individual content directly in the app (library or title view).
  • Remove all local data via the Android settings (Settings → Apps → Living Tales → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

Note: clearing the app data or uninstalling means losing access to your anonymous account — any remaining balance (Tales) cannot be restored, and the one-time welcome balance is granted only once per account.

2. Server data (anonymous account)

Server-side we store, under your random anonymous identifier (no name, no email): subscription status, usage and quota counters (Tales), technical usage events (numbers/metadata only, no content), and technical checksums (fingerprints without content) for the voicing binding; book content is not stored on our server.

  • We delete the remaining server data on request: write to the email address stated in the Legal Notice (under Privacy in the settings, "Request deletion of analytics data" opens a prepared email). We delete the associated data promptly, at the latest within 30 days.

The easiest way is to submit the request via the in-app report feature — your anonymous account identifier is sent along automatically, so we can match the server data. Alternatively you can write to us by email; without the account identifier we may not be able to match server-side data.

3. Subscription and payments

The Premium subscription and Tales packs are handled through Google Play; payment data is held by Google.
Cancelling the subscription or deleting your Google account only affects that Google data, not the app data listed above — delete the latter as described in sections 1 and 2.

4. Analytics data (only if consent was given)

If you consented to usage statistics or crash reports, you can withdraw consent at any time in the settings (collection stops immediately). To delete analytics data already collected, use the "request deletion of analytics data" entry in the settings, which starts an email request.
