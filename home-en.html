Your interactive audiobook generator

Your own audiobook — from any story you can imagine.

Describe a world — classic, fantasy, mystery, romance, a children's tale, whatever you like. Living Tales writes the book chapter by chapter and reads it to you, with a voice right on your device — offline and free.

How it works

  1. Choose a world — Genre, tone, length — or your own idea. You set the direction, or let the dice decide.
  2. The AI writes — Chapter after chapter, consistent, never forgetting. If you like, steer each chapter with a short hint before it's written.
  3. Your device reads it — Natural voice, offline and free. Listen whenever and wherever you like — resumable anytime.

What the app does

Any genre

Classic, fantasy, sci-fi, mystery, romance, horror, children's tales — or your own mix. No templates.

At your language level

From A1 to C2, vocabulary and sentences adapt: perfect for language learning, for young ears, or full literature at native depth.

Voice on your device

Text-to-speech right on your device — fully offline and free once generated.

As interactive as you want

Just listen — or step in before each chapter and steer the story. Your call.

Optional: a real LitRPG

If you like, unlock a full progression system: XP, stats, skills, loot, level-ups and relationships.

Private & in your hands

Books, settings and keys stay on your device. Bring your own key — or optional Premium with no keys at all.
