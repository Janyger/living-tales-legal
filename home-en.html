Interactive LitRPG audiobook generator

Create your story — and listen to it as an audiobook.

Living Tales turns a world you design into an ongoing, narrated adventure. You set the genre, hero and rules — the AI writes the next chapter, your device narrates it offline.

How it works

  1. Design your world — Set genre, protagonist, stats and tone — from a grim dungeon to a children's tale.
  2. Generate a chapter — One tap: the AI proposes the next chapter, and code validates every change against your world's rules.
  3. Listen offline — Finished audio plus transcript land on your device — playable without internet, resumable anytime.

What the app does

A living world

One structured world state: stats, skills and relationships evolve with every chapter.

Bring your own key

Your own AI key — stored encrypted on-device only. Data-minimal, no account required.

Any genre

Generic building blocks instead of genre templates: fantasy, sci-fi, romance, horror — or your own mix.

On-device voice

Text-to-speech right on your device, fully offline once generated.

You're in control

Generation happens only on your tap — never unprompted in the background.

Premium (optional)

Prefer no key of your own? The managed Premium mode handles the AI calls for you.
