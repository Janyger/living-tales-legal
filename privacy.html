Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how personal data is processed when using the app "Living Tales", in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of Art. 4 (7) GDPR is the entity named in the Legal Notice. Contact details are stated there.

2. Core principle: anonymous account, content on your device

Living Tales is designed to be data-minimising. There is no classic user account: no name, no email address, no password. The app signs your device in automatically with a random, anonymous identifier (Firebase Authentication, anonymous sign-in). Your finished books — text, audio files, cover and portrait images — are stored locally on your device and leave it only as far as required for generation or voicing (sections 3.2–3.4) or when you share/export them yourself.

3. Processing activities in detail

3.1 Anonymous account, quotas and abuse prevention

  • Data processed: random anonymous account identifier (Firebase Authentication), app integrity attestation (Firebase App Check / Play Integrity), subscription status and plan, usage and quota counters (in particular the Tales balance), the one-time welcome balance including its grant timestamp, and any blocking flags.
  • In addition, technical usage events are logged (e.g. type of call, model used, token/character counts, Tales spent) — numbers and metadata only, never your book content.
  • Purpose: operating the service, metering quotas, preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Storage location: Google Cloud Firestore (a project in Google's infrastructure).
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR (performance of contract) and Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in abuse prevention).

3.2 Content generation through our server

  • How it works: all AI calls made by the app (text generation, cover images, portraits, cloud voicing) are routed through our server service ("LT-Proxy", Google Cloud Run, region europe-west3 / Frankfurt am Main, Germany). There are no user-provided provider keys any more; the server holds the provider credentials.
  • Data processed: your inputs (world description, settings, hints), the existing story state and the prompts built from it, chapter text to be voiced, your anonymous account identifier, and technical connection data (e.g. IP address when contacting the server).
  • Recipients for text and images: for the actual model execution our server forwards the request to OpenRouter, Inc. (USA) as processor, and to AI data centres in the USA and the EU commissioned by OpenRouter.
  • Recipient for cloud voicing: Google (Google Gemini). The text to be spoken, the chosen voice identifier and a short delivery instruction per paragraph are transmitted.
  • The recipients process the data on the basis of their contractual and privacy terms (third-country transfer, see section 5).
  • Book content is not stored on our server; chapter text is transmitted for processing only. To technically bind voicing to your own, verified generation, only checksums (technical fingerprints without content) are stored, and they are overwritten upon re-registration.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR (providing the generation you requested).

3.3 Automated content moderation

  • How it works: text you enter freely (e.g. world descriptions, chapter hints) is automatically screened for prohibited content before generation. For this, the entered text is transmitted to a moderation model through the same service (OpenRouter, see section 3.2). If this service is not reachable, a simple filter on our server performs the check. Processing takes place on the basis of OpenRouter's contractual and privacy terms (third-country transfer, see section 5).
  • Repeated violations can lead to the account being blocked (section 3.1).
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in preventing unlawful use) and compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6 (1)(c) GDPR).

3.4 Voice features (no voice recording!)

  • Catalog voices: you choose the narrator voice from a catalog of ready-made, entirely synthetic voices. No recording of your voice is made and no voice clone is created.
  • Character voices: for book characters the app automatically assigns catalog voices (based on the character sheet, no free text). For voicing and previews only the text to be spoken, the chosen voice identifier and a short delivery instruction are transmitted to Google (Gemini speech synthesis); no voices are created or stored at the provider.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR.

3.5 Payment (Google Play)

  • The Premium subscription and one-time Tales packs are purchased and billed through Google Play. Payment data (e.g. payment method, billing details) is processed by Google; we do not receive it. Our server only receives the purchase token from the app and verifies it against the Google Play Developer API to attach the subscription status or purchased Tales to your anonymous account.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR.

3.6 Local storage

  • Data processed: app settings, generated chapters, audio files, cover/portrait images, library and progress data.
  • Purpose: app functionality (playback, continuation, management). Storage on the device only; backups/exports are created and shared by you.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR.

3.7 Analytics and crash reports (only with consent)

  • Services: Firebase Analytics (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash/error reports) provided by Google Ireland Ltd.
  • Purpose: stability and improvement of the app.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(a) GDPR (consent). You grant it separately and voluntarily during onboarding; without consent these services are not activated. You can withdraw consent at any time with future effect in the settings.

3.8 Configuration and legal-text delivery

The app fetches configuration values and the current versions of the legal documents via Firebase Remote Config (Google). Technical connection data (e.g. IP address) is processed for this; no content and no identifiers beyond the Firebase installation ID are transmitted.

3.9 App permissions

The app requires internet access (generation, account, subscription) and, optionally, the notification permission (generation progress, player controls). These permissions serve only the stated purposes.

3.10 Support and premium requests by email

An email address is processed only if you yourself actively send a request by email (e.g. support, deletion request). We process it solely to handle your request (Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR).

4. Recipients and third parties

  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC: Firebase Authentication (anonymous), App Check, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, Cloud Run (server location Frankfurt am Main), Google Play (subscription and Tales packs), Gemini speech synthesis (cloud voicing) and — only with consent — Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • OpenRouter, Inc. (USA) as processor for text, image and moderation requests, and AI data centres in the USA and the EU commissioned by OpenRouter (see sections 3.2 and 3.3).

These parties process the transmitted data under their own privacy policies or on the basis of our agreements with them.

5. Transfers to third countries

Our server service is operated in the EU (Google Cloud Run, Frankfurt am Main). Model execution and content moderation via OpenRouter as well as cloud voicing via Google take place in whole or in part in the USA. Transfers to the USA are based on appropriate safeguards within the meaning of Art. 44 et seq. GDPR (in particular standard contractual clauses provided by the providers) and, where necessary, on the performance of the contract (Art. 49 (1)(b) GDPR).

6. Retention period

Local content remains stored until you delete it in the app or by uninstalling. Account data (anonymous identifier, subscription status, counters) is stored as long as necessary for operation and abuse prevention. Book content is not stored on our server; the checksums stored for the voicing binding (fingerprints without content) are overwritten upon re-registration. Analytics data is processed according to the respective service's retention periods.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and to object (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent given at any time with future effect (Art. 7 (3) GDPR). A message to the contact address stated in the Legal Notice is sufficient to exercise these rights. How to delete local content, server data and analytics data is described on the "Data deletion" page:
https://janyger.github.io/living-tales-legal/deletion.html You can also request deletion at any time by email to the address stated in the Legal Notice.

8. Right to lodge a complaint

Without prejudice to any other remedy, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the member state of your residence.

9. No automated decision with legal effect

There is no solely automated decision producing legal effects concerning you (Art. 22 GDPR). Automated content moderation can block individual generations; a permanent account block is not imposed on a solely automated basis. If your account is paused automatically, you can request a review by a human at any time — via the in-app report feature or by email; every block is reviewed by us manually.

10. Minors

The app is not directed at children. Adult content is accessible only after a corresponding selection; please observe the age limits.

11. Changes to this policy

We adapt this policy when the processing or the legal situation changes. The current version can always be viewed in the app.
