Data Deletion

As of: June 2026

Living Tales is built to be data-minimal. Here is what data is stored where, and how you delete it.

1. Local data (default / bring-your-own-key)

In standard operation all content is stored exclusively on your device: your API keys (encrypted), app settings, generated chapters, audio files, cover images and library/progress data. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

  • Delete individual content directly in the app (library or title view).
  • Remove all local data via Android settings (Settings → Apps → Living Tales → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

2. Premium data (server-side)

If you use the optional Premium mode, we store server-side a random, anonymous identifier (no name, no email) plus usage and quota counters required for operation and abuse prevention. To delete this data:

  • In the app: Settings → "Delete my data". This removes the server data stored for your anonymous identifier and resets the identifier.
  • Fallback without the app: email us at the address listed in the Imprint and include the device identifier shown in the app under Settings. We will delete the associated data promptly, at the latest within 30 days.

3. Subscription and payments

Any Premium subscription is handled through Google Play; payment data resides with Google. Cancelling the subscription or deleting your Google account only affects that Google data, not the app data described above — delete the latter as described in sections 1 and 2.
